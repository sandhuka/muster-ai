# Monetization Skill

Research pricing strategies, revenue models, and retention benchmarks for your product category.

## Revenue Models

Evaluate which model fits this product:

- **Freemium**: Free tier with limited features or content, paid unlocks full access + premium capabilities. Most common in content-driven apps.
- **Subscription**: Monthly/annual. Industry norm varies by category ($5-15/mo or $30-80/yr typical). Higher retention with annual.
- **One-time purchase**: Rare for content-heavy apps. May work for bundled packs or tools.
- **Hybrid**: Free core + paid premium features/content + optional one-time specialty packs.

Research what price points work in your market and what users are willing to pay (use review data, competitor pricing, survey data if available).

## Conversion Benchmarks

Typical app metrics to research for your category:

- Free-to-paid conversion rate (industry benchmarks for your category)
- Trial-to-paid conversion (industry: 15-30% for 7-day trials)
- Monthly vs annual subscription split
- Average revenue per user (ARPU)

## Retention & Churn

Research retention benchmarks for your product category:

- Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30 retention rates
- Average subscription churn (monthly vs annual)
- What drives retention: streaks, reminders, progress tracking, social, content freshness?
- What causes churn: content staleness, lack of variety, no results tracking, price

## Paywall Strategy

When and where to gate content:

- After onboarding (let user experience one core activity free)?
- Feature-gated (free core experience but no personalization/advanced features)?
- Content-gated (limited content categories free, full library paid)?
- Time-gated (7-day free trial, then paywall)?

Research which approach has highest conversion for your product category specifically.

## Output Format
Document findings in `knowledge-base/research/monetization.md` using the standard research doc structure:
- **Current State**: 5-10 line summary
- **Findings**: Pricing models, revenue projections, conversion benchmarks
- **Open Questions**: What needs further investigation
- **History**: Dated one-liner entries for major updates

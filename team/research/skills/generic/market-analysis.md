# Market Analysis Skill

Size the home fitness app market, identify trends, and map opportunities.

## Market Sizing

- **TAM**: Total home fitness / digital fitness market globally. Use industry reports + bottom-up (smartphone users × fitness app adoption rate × avg spend).
- **SAM**: Narrow by platform (iOS + Android), geography (English-speaking markets), and positioning (home workout, no-gym, minimal equipment).
- **SOM**: Realistic Year 1 and Year 3 capture. Factor in app store discoverability, marketing budget, and competitive density.
- **Sources**: Statista, Grand View Research, Sensor Tower, data.ai, App Annie, public earnings from competitors (Peloton, Lululemon/Mirror).

## Trends to Track

- Post-COVID home fitness retention vs gym return rates
- AI/personalization in fitness apps
- Short-form video fitness content (TikTok influence on workout formats)
- Wearable integration (Apple Watch, Fitbit) as engagement driver
- Mental wellness convergence (breathwork, yoga, meditation bundled with fitness)

## Category Analysis

Classify the category: emerging / growing / mature / declining. Map key players by segment (video-led, AI coach, social fitness, yoga-specific, HIIT-specific). Note barriers to entry and switching costs.

## Technology Opportunities

Relevant APIs/SDKs: HealthKit, Google Fit, video streaming/CDN, exercise recognition (pose estimation), notification/habit systems. Flag emerging tech competitors haven't adopted.

## Output Format
Document findings in `knowledge-base/research/market-landscape.md` using the standard research doc structure:
- **Current State**: 5-10 line summary
- **Findings**: Detailed analysis by sub-topic
- **Open Questions**: What needs further investigation
- **History**: Dated one-liner entries for major updates

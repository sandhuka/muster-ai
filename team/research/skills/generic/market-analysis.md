# Market Analysis Skill

Size your product category market, identify trends, and map opportunities.

## Market Sizing

- **TAM**: Total addressable market globally for your product category. Use industry reports + bottom-up (target platform users × category adoption rate × avg spend).
- **SAM**: Narrow by platform (iOS, Android, web), geography (target markets), and positioning (your specific niche within the category).
- **SOM**: Realistic Year 1 and Year 3 capture. Factor in marketplace discoverability, marketing budget, and competitive density.
- **Sources**: Statista, Grand View Research, Sensor Tower, data.ai, App Annie, public earnings from publicly traded competitors.

## Trends to Track

- Macro trends affecting your product category (regulatory shifts, consumer behavior changes, technology adoption curves)
- AI/personalization trends relevant to your domain
- Content format shifts (short-form video, interactive media, social integration)
- Platform integration opportunities (wearables, smart devices, companion apps)
- Adjacent category convergence (related verticals bundling overlapping features)

## Category Analysis

Classify the category: emerging / growing / mature / declining. Map key players by segment (content-led, AI-driven, social, niche-specific). Note barriers to entry and switching costs.

## Technology Opportunities

Research relevant APIs/SDKs for your domain: platform health/data frameworks, media delivery, recognition/detection technologies, notification/habit systems. Flag emerging tech competitors haven't adopted.

## Output Format
Document findings in `knowledge-base/research/market-landscape.md` using the standard research doc structure:
- **Current State**: 5-10 line summary
- **Findings**: Detailed analysis by sub-topic
- **Open Questions**: What needs further investigation
- **History**: Dated one-liner entries for major updates

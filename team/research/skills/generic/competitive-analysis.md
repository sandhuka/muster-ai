# Competitive Analysis Skill

Tear down competitors in your product category, identify gaps, find differentiation angles.

## Competitor Categories

- **Direct**: Products solving the same core problem for the same audience with a similar approach
- **Indirect**: Alternative solutions users cobble together (free tools, manual processes, general-purpose platforms)
- **Adjacent**: Products in related categories that could expand into your space, or that your users also use
- **Emerging**: Startups and new entrants using novel approaches (AI-powered, community-driven, platform-native)

## Feature Matrix

Compare across the features most relevant to your product category. For each feature, mark as: **table stakes** (everyone has it), **differentiator** (1-2 have it), or **missing** (nobody does it well).

Include dimensions like: core experience quality, personalization depth, content/asset library, onboarding, free tier generosity, platform integration, offline access, social features, data portability.

## Gap Analysis

- Who offers truly personalized experiences? (most don't — they offer configuration, not personalization)
- Who handles your specific use case well vs. just offering a generic solution?
- Where is the UX frustrating? (check app store reviews for complaints)
- What integrations are missing?
- Which user segments are underserved?

## Pricing Landscape

For each major competitor: free tier scope, paid price, pricing model (subscription/freemium/one-time), what triggers conversion. Note the gap between free alternatives and premium paid products.

## Differentiation Angle

Based on findings, articulate where your product can win. State the core hypothesis and use competitive evidence to validate or challenge it.

## Output Format
Document findings in `knowledge-base/research/competitive-analysis.md` using the standard research doc structure:
- **Current State**: 5-10 line summary
- **Findings**: Competitor profiles, feature matrix, gaps
- **Open Questions**: What needs further investigation
- **History**: Dated one-liner entries for major updates

# Competitive Analysis Skill

Tear down competitors in the home fitness app space, identify gaps, find differentiation angles.

## Competitor Categories

- **Direct**: Home workout apps with video routines (Nike Training Club, FitOn, Freeletics, Fitbod, JEFIT)
- **Indirect**: YouTube fitness channels, Instagram/TikTok creators selling programs
- **Adjacent**: Yoga-specific (Down Dog, Alo Moves), meditation (Calm, Headspace with fitness), smart equipment (Peloton, Tonal, Mirror)
- **Emerging**: AI-powered fitness startups, personalized routine builders

## Feature Matrix

Compare across: routine personalization, exercise video library size, science-backed programming, muscle group scheduling, recovery guidance, equipment flexibility (none/minimal/full), workout duration options, progress tracking, wearable integration, offline access, social features.

Mark each as: **table stakes** (everyone has it), **differentiator** (1-2 have it), or **missing** (nobody does it well).

## Gap Analysis

- Who offers truly personalized weekly programming with recovery logic? (most don't)
- Who handles the "minimal equipment at home" use case well vs just dumping a video library?
- Where is the UX frustrating? (check app store reviews)
- What integrations are missing?
- Which user segments are underserved? (beginners, time-constrained, multi-discipline)

## Pricing Landscape

For each major competitor: free tier scope, paid price, pricing model (sub/freemium/one-time), what triggers conversion. Note the gap between free YouTube content and $15-30/mo premium apps.

## Differentiation Angle

Based on findings, articulate where our product can win. The smart routine builder (science-backed weekly programming from micro-videos) is the hypothesis — validate or challenge it.

## Output Format
Document findings in `knowledge-base/research/competitive-analysis.md` using the standard research doc structure:
- **Current State**: 5-10 line summary
- **Findings**: Competitor profiles, feature matrix, gaps
- **Open Questions**: What needs further investigation
- **History**: Dated one-liner entries for major updates

# SEO Guidelines

## Purpose
Define SEO standards for web content. See the `app-store-listing` skill for App Store Optimization (ASO). See the `content-calendar` skill for content strategy and publishing cadence. See `knowledge-base/brand-guidelines.md` for voice consistency across SEO content.

## SEO Strategy

### Keyword Targeting
- **Primary terms**: [3-5 terms that describe your core product in user language — what would someone search to find an app like yours?]
- **Long-tail focus**: Lower competition, higher intent. Target phrases that describe the user's *problem*, not your *solution* — e.g., "[app] that [solves specific pain point]," "[category] app for [target persona]"
- **Intent mapping**: Match content type to search intent — informational (blog), navigational (landing page), transactional (download CTA)
- **Avoid**: Broad single-word category terms — unwinnable for a new brand. Target specific pain points instead

### Content Tiers

| Tier | Type | Word Count | Purpose | Example Topics |
|------|------|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Pillar | Comprehensive guide | 1500-2500 | Rank for primary terms, hub for clusters | "[Complete Guide to [Your Core Topic]]" |
| Cluster | Supporting article | 800-1200 | Rank for long-tail, link to pillar | "[Specific subtopic under the pillar]" |
| Short | Quick answer | 400-600 | Featured snippet / FAQ targeting | "[Common question your users Google]" |

### E-E-A-T (Especially for YMYL Categories)
Google applies stricter quality standards to YMYL content (Your Money or Your Life — health, finance, education, legal):
- **Experience**: Reference real user scenarios and pain points your product addresses
- **Expertise**: Cite authoritative sources in your domain ([relevant professional organizations, research bodies])
- **Authoritativeness**: Link to product research and methodology pages, build topical authority through content clusters
- **Trust**: Include [domain] disclaimers on all [regulated]-adjacent content, link to privacy policy, show transparent author/brand information

If your product is NOT in a YMYL category, E-E-A-T still matters for rankings — but the bar is lower. Focus on Experience and Expertise.

## On-Page SEO Standards

### Per-Page Requirements
- Title tag: 50-60 characters, primary keyword near front, brand name at end
- Meta description: 150-160 characters, clear value proposition, action-oriented
- One H1 per page matching search intent
- H2-H3 subheadings with natural keyword inclusion — never stuffed
- Image alt text: descriptive, includes keywords naturally
- Internal links: every article links to 2-3 related pieces and one pillar page

### Domain Disclaimers
- If your product is in a regulated domain, every blog post or web page making [domain] claims must include a condensed disclaimer
- Placement: end of article or sidebar — visible, not hidden
- Language must match Legal agent guidelines

## Technical SEO

- Mobile-first: all content must be fully functional on mobile
- Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms
- Structured data: Schema.org for articles (`Article`, `HowTo`, `FAQPage`)
- Canonical URLs on all pages
- XML sitemap submitted and auto-updated

## Principles

1. **Pain points over keywords**: Write for the user's problem and optimize for search second. Keyword-first content reads like SEO spam and contradicts your brand voice.
2. **YMYL compliance if applicable**: If Google treats your category as health/finance/education-adjacent, every article needs E-E-A-T signals and disclaimers — not because it's nice, but because rankings depend on it.
3. **Cluster depth beats publishing frequency**: One pillar + 5 supporting articles outperforms 10 disconnected blog posts. Build topical authority systematically.

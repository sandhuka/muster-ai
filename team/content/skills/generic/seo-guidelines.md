# SEO Guidelines

## Purpose
Define SEO standards for web content targeting the fitness and wellness space. See `team/content/skills/app-store-listing.md` for App Store Optimization (ASO). See `team/content/skills/content-calendar.md` for content strategy and publishing cadence. See `knowledge-base/brand-guidelines.md` for voice consistency across SEO content.

## Fitness Content SEO Strategy

### Keyword Targeting
- **Primary terms**: Smart fitness app, personalized workout app, daily workout routine, strength yoga stretching app
- **Long-tail focus**: Lower competition, higher intent. Examples: "workout app that tells you what to do," "fitness app for busy professionals," "app that combines strength and yoga"
- **Intent mapping**: Match content type to search intent — informational (blog), navigational (landing page), transactional (download CTA)
- **Avoid**: Broad terms like "fitness" or "workout" — unwinnable for a new brand. Target specific pain points instead

### Content Tiers

| Tier | Type | Word Count | Purpose | Example Topics |
|------|------|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Pillar | Comprehensive guide | 1500-2500 | Rank for primary terms, hub for clusters | "Complete Guide to Home Strength Training Without Equipment" |
| Cluster | Supporting article | 800-1200 | Rank for long-tail, link to pillar | "5 Bodyweight Exercises for Upper Body Strength" |
| Short | Quick answer | 400-600 | Featured snippet / FAQ targeting | "How Long Should You Rest Between Sets?" |

### E-E-A-T for Health/Fitness
Google applies stricter quality standards to health-adjacent content (YMYL — Your Money or Your Life):
- **Experience**: Reference real workout scenarios and user pain points (decision fatigue, program rigidity)
- **Expertise**: Cite exercise science sources (ACSM, NSCA) for training methodology claims
- **Authoritativeness**: Link to product research and methodology pages, build topical authority through content clusters
- **Trust**: Include fitness disclaimers on all health-adjacent content, link to privacy policy, show transparent author/brand information

## On-Page SEO Standards

### Per-Page Requirements
- Title tag: 50-60 characters, primary keyword near front, brand name at end
- Meta description: 150-160 characters, clear value proposition, action-oriented
- One H1 per page matching search intent
- H2-H3 subheadings with natural keyword inclusion — never stuffed
- Image alt text: descriptive, includes keywords naturally
- Internal links: every article links to 2-3 related pieces and one pillar page

### Health Content Disclaimers
- Every blog post or web page making fitness claims must include a condensed health disclaimer
- Placement: end of article or sidebar — visible, not hidden
- Language must match Legal agent guidelines (see `team/legal/skills/fitness-claims-advertising.md`)

## Technical SEO

- Mobile-first: all content must be fully functional on mobile
- Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms
- Structured data: Schema.org for articles (`Article`, `HowTo`, `FAQPage`)
- Canonical URLs on all pages
- XML sitemap submitted and auto-updated

## Principles

1. **Pain points over keywords**: Write for the user's problem ("I don't know what to do today") and optimize for search second. Keyword-first content reads like SEO spam and contradicts the Intelligent Coach voice.
2. **YMYL compliance is non-negotiable**: Google treats fitness content as health-adjacent. Every article needs E-E-A-T signals and disclaimers — not because it's nice, but because rankings depend on it.
3. **Cluster depth beats publishing frequency**: One pillar + 5 supporting articles outperforms 10 disconnected blog posts. Build topical authority systematically.

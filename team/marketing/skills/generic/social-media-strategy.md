# Social Media Strategy

## Purpose
Platform-by-platform playbook for organic social media. Social is the primary awareness engine for a consumer app. This skill covers platform mechanics, content strategy, and cadence. Coordinate with Content agent (`team/content/skills/brand-voice.md`) for copy/voice consistency. See `team/marketing/skills/paid-acquisition.md` for amplifying organic winners with paid spend.

## Platform Priority (Solo Founder)

| Priority | Platform | Time/Week | Start When | Primary Goal |
|----------|----------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| 1 | TikTok | 3-4 hrs | 6 weeks pre-launch | Reach + awareness |
| 2 | Instagram | 3-4 hrs | 6 weeks pre-launch | Brand + community |
| 3 | Reddit | 1-2 hrs | 8 weeks pre-launch | Credibility seeding |
| 4 | YouTube Shorts | 1 hr | Launch | Repurpose TikTok |
| 5 | Twitter/X | 1 hr | Pre-launch | Indie dev network |

Master platforms sequentially. Total: ~10-12 hrs/week for platforms 1-5.

## TikTok

**Algorithm**: Completion rate is king. Each video is tested on ~200-500 people; high completion → shown to 2K → 10K → 100K+. Follower count barely matters.

**Content formats**: Quick tips ("[N things your audience is doing wrong]"), POV ("[your app] actually [solves the pain point]"), screen recordings, myth-busting, time-lapses of [core experience].

**Hooks (first 1.5 sec)**: Bold statement, direct question, or mid-action start. Never "Hey guys, so today..."

| Element | Guidance |
|---------|---------|
| Cadence | 1/day ideal, 5/week minimum |
| Length | 15-30 sec sweet spot |
| Hashtags | 3-5 per post: 1 broad (#[category]), 2 medium (#[subcategory]), 1-2 niche (#[differentiator]). No #fyp |
| Sound | Trending when natural; original audio fine for educational content |
| Engagement | Respond within 1-2 hrs. Pin best comments. Reply with video when possible |
| Batching | Record 5-7 videos in one 1-2 hr session. Edit in CapCut |

**Branded hashtag**: #[ProductName][CoreAction] — use consistently; it compounds over time.

## Instagram

**Reels** (growth driver): Repurpose TikTok — remove watermark, adjust hashtags (15-20), adjust caption style. 1/day or 5/week.

**Stories** (engagement, not growth): Daily behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A. Use Link sticker to App Store. Nurtures existing followers.

**Feed** (brand building): 2-3x/week carousels (swipeable tips — highest save rate), product screenshots, testimonials. First slide = hook.

**Bio**: One-line value prop + social proof when available + "[Download/Try] free" CTA + App Store link. Highlights: "How It Works," "[Free vs Premium / Key Feature]," "[Results / Testimonials]."

**Hashtags**: 15-20 per post. Rotate 3-4 sets. Mix broad/medium/niche/community. Never the same set twice in a row.

## Reddit

**Core rule**: Reddit hates self-promotion. 10 helpful comments for every 1 self-mention. Build karma for 4-6 weeks before mentioning the app. When you do mention it, be transparent ("I built this").

**Target subreddits**: Identify 5-8 communities where your target users ask questions your product answers. Prioritize by:
- Size (100K+ for reach, 10K-100K for engagement depth)
- Relevance (users discussing the problem you solve)
- Self-promotion rules (some subs ban it entirely — read the sidebar)

Include r/SideProject (200K, launch posts allowed) and r/startups for indie dev audiences regardless of category.

**AMA strategy**: After establishing credibility, post "I built [one-line product description], AMA" in r/startups or r/SideProject.

**Never**: Multiple accounts, same link in multiple subs, astroturfing, arguing with critics.

## YouTube Shorts
Repurpose TikTok. Adjust: max 60 sec, add "#Shorts" in title, add end card. Post 3-5/week. Low additional effort.

**Long-form** (defer to 50K+ users): "[How your core feature works]" explainers, [guided sessions / tutorials]. SEO-optimized titles. 1/week minimum. Videos compound for years.

## Twitter/X
Building-in-public: milestones, challenges, decisions, metrics (selectively). Thread format 1/week. Engage indie dev + [your category] communities. 1-2 tweets/day + 10 conversations/week.

## Content Repurposing
One video → TikTok + Reels + YouTube Short + Twitter clip + Instagram Story. One blog post → 5 social snippets + 1 email + 1 thread + 1 carousel. Create once, distribute everywhere.

| TikTok | Views, profile visits, link clicks | 10K+ avg views/video |

## Trend-Riding
Decision filter before jumping on a trend: (1) connects naturally to your product's domain? (2) matches your brand voice and tone? (3) can create in <30 min? (4) would your target demographic engage? All four yes → make it fast. Any no → skip without guilt.

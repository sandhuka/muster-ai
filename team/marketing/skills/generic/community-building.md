# Community Building

## Purpose
Strategy for building a community that drives retention, UGC, and word-of-mouth. Community creates switching costs (social bonds), free content (UGC), and compounds all other marketing. At 100K+ users, a well-built community becomes self-sustaining. See `team/marketing/skills/referral-virality.md` for challenges and sharing hooks, `team/marketing/skills/creator-partnerships.md` for ambassador program crossover.

## Strategy by Growth Stage

| Stage | Philosophy | Actions | Time |
|-------|-----------|---------|------|
| 0-1K | Founder-led | Respond to every review, DM, comment. Personal relationships with every user | 3-5 hrs/week |
| 1K-10K | Curated | Launch Discord. Invite 50-100 most active users. Founder-led weekly discussions, feature polls, early access | 3-4 hrs/week |
| 10K-100K | Structured | Community guidelines, themed channels, weekly events, 3-5 moderators (free premium + early access) | 2-3 hrs/week |
| 100K+ | Scaled | Ambassador program, user-run challenges, UGC flywheel, community manager (part-time). Founder: monthly AMA | 1-2 hrs/week |

## Channel Selection

| Channel | When | Use For |
|---------|------|---------|
| Instagram comments/DMs | Day 1 | Lowest overhead, 1-on-1 conversations |
| Discord | 1K+ active users | Persistent group space, channels, roles. Structure: #general, #[core-activity], #feature-requests, #introductions |
| Reddit (r/[ProductName]) | 5K+ users | Don't create earlier — dead subreddits hurt. Until then, engage in [category subs] (see `social-media-strategy.md`) |
| In-app | Product roadmap | Ultimate destination. Defer until PMF proven. MVP: Challenge tab for seasonal events |

## Community Content

| Type | Cadence | Format |
|------|---------|--------|
| User spotlight | Weekly | Feature one member's [consistency/achievement] story (with consent). Post on Instagram + Discord |
| Challenges | Monthly | Themed: "[N]-Day [Seasonal Theme]," "[Focus Area] [Month]." Opt-in, daily check-ins, completion cards |
| Q&A / education | Weekly | "[Feature-specific Q&A]" — explain how [core product intelligence] works. Repurpose as social content |
| Behind-the-scenes | Ongoing | Dev decisions, feature previews, metrics (selectively). Makes users feel like insiders |

## Ambassador Program (Post-10K Users)

**Selection**: [N+ core actions] completed, 30+ days active, positive community engagement, genuine enthusiasm. Prefer content creators but not required. [Your platform] user.

| Benefit | Expectation |
|---------|------------|
| Lifetime premium | 2-4 posts/month mentioning the product naturally |
| Early feature access (TestFlight) | Community participation 3x/week |
| Monthly founder group call | Beta testing + honest feedback |
| Input on product decisions | Report bugs proactively |

**Size**: 5-10 ambassadors, max 20 until 100K users. If inactive 60+ days, gracefully offboard.
**Management**: monthly group call (30-60 min) + weekly Discord message (15 min) + quarterly review (1-2 hrs) = ~3-4 hrs/month.

## UGC Strategy
- **Branded hashtag**: #My[ProductName]Routine — use consistently on all content. Monitor weekly for repost opportunities
- **Best UGC strategy**: make the product naturally shareable (beautiful share cards, milestone celebrations). Users post without being asked
- **When requesting**: after positive review/NPS, in community challenges, from ambassadors
- **Rights**: always get written permission before reposting. Credit the creator. See `creator-partnerships.md` for formal rights management

## Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Weekly active engagement | 5%+ of members active/week |
| Community-driven installs | Track via UTMs + community referral codes |
| UGC volume | 10+ branded hashtag posts/month |
| Toxicity incidents | <1/month |

## Principles
1. **Give before you ask** — community serves users first. Marketing benefits are the byproduct
2. **Small and engaged > large and dead** — 200 active members beats 5,000 silent ones
3. **Protect culture** — enforce guidelines consistently. Remove bad actors quickly
4. **Match brand tone** — supportive, knowledgeable, no-BS. Actively exclude culture that contradicts your brand values

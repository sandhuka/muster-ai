# Creator & Influencer Partnerships

## Purpose
Strategy for identifying, partnering with, and measuring creators in your category. Micro-influencers deliver 3-5x ROAS — one well-matched creator can drive 500-2K installs per post. See `team/marketing/skills/paid-acquisition.md` for repurposing creator content as Spark/Partnership Ads. Coordinate with Legal agent for FTC compliance and Content agent (`team/content/skills/brand-voice.md`) for brand consistency.

## Creator Tiers

| Tier | Followers | Compensation | Volume Target |
|------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Nano | 1K-10K | Free premium (6-12 months) | 20-50 creators |
| Micro | 10K-100K | $200-500/post or rev-share | 10-20 creators |
| Mid | 100K-500K | $500-2K/post | 3-5 creators |
| Macro | 500K+ | $2K-10K/post (defer to 100K+ users) | 1-2 creators |

**Solo founder rule**: Start with nano product seeding + 3-5 micro partnerships. No mid/macro until micro playbook is proven.

## Ideal Creator Profile

**Good fit signals**: Creates content in your product's niche, educational/authentic tone, iOS users (if iOS-first), engaged comments (real conversations not just emoji), audience demographics match your target user.

**Bad fit signals**: Audience doesn't overlap with your target user, tone contradicts your brand voice (e.g., aggressive when you're calm), relies on claims you can't make (health, financial, etc.), fake engagement (<1% rate), wrong platform (Android if you're iOS-only).

## Discovery & Outreach

**Finding creators**: Instagram hashtag search ([your category's top 3-4 hashtags]), TikTok Creator Marketplace, competitors' tagged posts (who promotes your top 3 competitors?), your own engaged followers.

**Outreach** (10-15 messages/week during recruitment):
- Cold email (micro+): Reference specific content, explain the product in one sentence, offer free premium to try, no pressure
- Cold DM (nano): Shorter version, same approach
- Follow up once after 5 days. No second follow-up — move on

**Response rates**: Cold email 10-15%, cold DM 5-10%, mutual connection 30-40%. Of responses, ~25% will actually post.

## Collaboration Formats

| Format | Conversion | Best For |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Integration video | Highest | Creator shows [core experience] using the product |
| Honest review | High | Give full access, ask for genuine review |
| "Day in my life" | High | App appears naturally in routine |
| 7-day challenge | Very high (sustained) | Daily content over a week |

## Deal Structures

| Structure | When | Pros | Cons |
|-----------|------|------|------|
| Product-for-post | Nano, initial tests | Zero cost | Low commitment |
| Flat fee | Proven micro | Predictable | No performance alignment |
| Rev-share (20-30% first month) | Ongoing partnerships | Aligned incentives | Needs tracking |
| Hybrid (small fee + rev-share) | Primary micro deals | Best alignment | Slightly complex |

Track via unique referral code per creator. Attribute for 30 days after last click. Monthly payment, $25 minimum payout.

## Creator Brief (Send After Agreement)
Keep light — don't over-prescribe:
- About the product: one paragraph (key value proposition and differentiators)
- Key messages (pick 1-2): [Your top 2-3 differentiators phrased as user benefits, not features]
- What NOT to say: no [regulated claims in your domain], no [statements that misrepresent your free tier], no [common misconceptions about your product]
- Requirements: #ad or #sponsored disclosure, App Store link, unique referral code
- Creative freedom: "This is YOUR content for YOUR audience"

## UGC Collection & Rights
- Get written permission BEFORE repurposing creator content (repost, paid ads)
- Specify in agreement: repost on product channels, use as paid creative, 12-month duration
- **Spark Ads** (TikTok): boost creator's organic post directly
- **Partnership Ads** (Meta): run from creator's account (higher trust)
- Maintain organized UGC library: creator, date, rights doc, performance data, expiration

## FTC & Legal
- All paid partnerships: #ad or #sponsored at BEGINNING of caption (not buried)
- Video: verbal disclosure in first 30 seconds AND written in description
- Free product/premium counts as compensation — still requires disclosure
- No specific health/weight claims. Flag any health claims to Legal agent BEFORE posting
- Instagram: use "Paid partnership" tag

## Performance Tracking

| Metric | Target (Micro) |
|--------|---------------|
| Installs per post | 50-200 |
| CPA | $5-15 |
| Trial starts | 20%+ of installs |
| D7 retention | >15% (within 80% of organic baseline) |

Review per-creator metrics within 72 hours of each post. Monthly compare all creators by CPA. Quarterly: renew, upgrade to ambassador, or let go.

## Scaling Phases
1. **0-10K users**: 5-10 nano + 3-5 micro. Product-for-post. Learn what works (3-4 hrs/week)
2. **10K-50K**: 10-20 micro. Paid + rev-share. Repeatable pipeline (4-5 hrs/week)
3. **50K-100K**: 3-5 long-term ambassadors + micro pipeline. See `community-building.md` (3-4 hrs/week)
4. **100K+**: Expand to mid-tier. Launch self-serve affiliate program (5-6 hrs/week)

# Growth Strategy Framework

## Purpose
Full-funnel growth strategy: growth milestones, unit economics, freemium positioning, and channel prioritization. This is the strategic backbone — it answers "what to focus on and when." See individual skill files for tactical execution of each channel and strategy.

## Funnel Stages & Metrics

| Stage | Key Metrics |
|-------|------------|
| Awareness | Impressions, reach, brand search volume, followers |
| Acquisition | Downloads, install-to-signup rate, CAC by channel |
| Activation | Onboarding completion, [first core action] completion, time-to-value |
| Retention | D1/D7/D30, WAU/MAU, [core actions]/user/week |
| Revenue | Trial-to-paid conversion, MRR, LTV, churn, annual vs. monthly mix |
| Referral | Viral coefficient (k), referral conversion, NPS, invite rate |

## Growth Milestones

### 0 → 1K Users (Weeks 1-8)
**Focus**: Product-market fit signal. **Spend**: $0-500/mo (sweat equity).
Tactics: Personal outreach, beta tester conversion, founder's network, community seeding in [your category's top forums/subreddits], 1-2 micro-influencer partnerships, building-in-public on Twitter/X.
**Signal to advance**: D7 >15%, [core actions]/user/week >[habit threshold], 40%+ "disappointed if gone" in survey.

### 1K → 10K (Months 2-6)
**Focus**: Find 2-3 repeatable channels. **Spend**: $500-2K/mo.
Tactics: Scale Apple Search Ads, organic social (TikTok + Reels), email sequences, 5-10 micro-influencers, editorial submission, first PR push.
**Signal to advance**: Consistent weekly growth, CAC <$[target] on Apple Search Ads, [conversion target]%+ conversion, 4.5+ stars with 200+ reviews.

### 10K → 100K (Months 6-18)
**Focus**: Scale proven channels, add paid social. **Spend**: $2K-10K/mo.
Tactics: Scale Apple Search Ads, launch Meta ads, creator program, referral program, seasonal campaigns, community building.
**Signal to advance**: Blended CAC <$[target], D30 >10%, MRR growing 15%+/month, k-factor >0.15.

### 100K → 1M (Months 12-36)
**Focus**: Multi-channel scale, brand building. **Spend**: $10K-50K/mo.
Tactics: Full paid stack, mid-tier creators, ambassador program, community challenges, PR/thought leadership, localization.
**Signal**: Organic installs >40% of total, community generates UGC unprompted, k-factor >0.3.

## Principles
1. **Measure before scaling** — 7+ days of data and a CPA target before increasing spend. See `analytics.md`
2. **Free tier must feel complete** — if free users feel tricked, they churn, they don't upgrade
3. **Organic before paid** — paid amplifies what works organically, it doesn't fix what doesn't
4. **Surface value at natural moments** — upgrade prompts at value moments, not as interruptive modals
5. **Compound over campaign** — prioritize SEO, community, referral (compound) over paid (stops when you stop)
6. **Solo founder discipline** — fewer things done well beats many things done poorly

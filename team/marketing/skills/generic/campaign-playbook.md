# Campaign Playbook

## Purpose
Campaign orchestration — how to plan, execute, and evaluate marketing campaigns across channels. Individual channel tactics live in dedicated skill files. Coordinate with Content agent (`team/content/skills/brand-voice.md`) for copy, Legal agent for compliance review on claims. See `knowledge-base/brand-guidelines.md` for brand tone.

## Campaign Planning Template
Every campaign answers these 10 questions before launch:

1. **Objective**: Exactly one — awareness / acquisition / activation / retention / revenue / referral
2. **Audience**: Segment with demographics, psychographics, current behavior
3. **Message**: One-sentence core value proposition for this audience
4. **Channels**: Max 2-3 channels per campaign
5. **Budget**: Total + per-channel allocation with expected CPA
6. **Timeline**: Start, milestones, end
7. **Creative**: Assets needed + which agent delivers (Content for copy, UI/UX for visuals)
8. **Legal review**: Flag [regulated domain] claims BEFORE launch. No exceptions
9. **KPIs**: 2-3 measurable metrics with specific targets
10. **Reporting**: When/how results will be reviewed

## Campaign Types

| Type | Duration | Budget Share | Examples |
|------|----------|-------------|---------|
| Always-on | Continuous | 50-60% | Apple Search Ads, email lifecycle, organic social, ASO |
| Burst | 1-2 weeks | 10-15% | App launch, feature releases, milestones |
| Seasonal | 2-4 weeks | 20-25% | January push, September reset, Black Friday. See `seasonal-cultural-marketing.md` |
| Lifecycle | Continuous (triggered) | 5-10% | Welcome, re-engagement, win-back. See `retention-lifecycle.md` |

## Creative Brief Template
Marketing fills this, hands to Content + UI/UX agents:

- **Campaign name + objective**: one line
- **Target audience**: who, demographics, behavior
- **Key message**: one sentence
- **Tone**: reference `brand-guidelines.md` + any campaign-specific adjustments
- **Deliverables**: asset type, specs (dimensions, length, format)
- **Channels**: where assets will appear
- **Constraints**: legal flags, brand dos/don'ts, deadline
- **References**: similar content, competitor examples, past winners

## Campaign Post-Mortem Template
Run after every burst and seasonal campaign. Document in `knowledge-base/decision-log.md`:

- **Dates, objective, budget spent**
- **Results table**: KPI / Target / Actual / Assessment (met, missed, exceeded)
- **What worked**: specific tactic, creative, or channel
- **What didn't work**: specific underperformer
- **Key learnings**: insight for future campaigns
- **Next steps**: follow-up actions

## Solo Founder Checklist

### Before (2-3 hrs)
- [ ] Brief filled (10 questions), creative ready, legal reviewed, tracking in place, budget capped

### During (30 min/day)
- [ ] Check spend + CPA daily. Respond to engagement within 2 hrs. Pause any ad set >2x CPA after $50

### After (1-2 hrs)
- [ ] Pull metrics, complete post-mortem, log learnings, archive creative
